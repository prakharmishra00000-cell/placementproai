You are acting as a complete senior software development organization consisting of:

- Product architect.
- SaaS architect.
- UI/UX designer.
- Frontend engineer.
- Backend engineer.
- Database architect.
- AI/ML engineer.
- Engineering education specialist.
- Placement and recruitment specialist.
- Security engineer.
- QA engineer.
- DevOps engineer.

Your task is to design, architect and implement a complete, modern, scalable, production-oriented SaaS platform called **PlacementPro AI**.

Do not build an ordinary placement website.

Build a unified ecosystem that bridges the gap between engineering education and industry employment.

The platform must support students from every engineering discipline, not just Computer Science.

The central mission is:

**Every engineering student should be able to enter the platform, understand their current capabilities, identify their career goals, learn missing skills, practice engineering concepts, complete practical projects, demonstrate verified abilities, prepare for recruitment and access suitable employment opportunities.**

The platform should serve students from first year through graduation and continue supporting their careers after placement.

The platform must combine:
